Example 1.2.1: Projecting Vector v [2, 4] onto Line a [1, 1]

Notice the perpendicular drop: line y = x has slope +1, and drop line e has slope -1. Their product is -1, confirming the 90° angle. Hover over points to inspect coordinates.

Vector v: [2, 4] (Original)
Direction a: [1, 1] (Diagonal line)
Projection p: [3, 3] (Shadow)
Error e: [-1, 1] (Slope = -1, Orthogonal drop)
