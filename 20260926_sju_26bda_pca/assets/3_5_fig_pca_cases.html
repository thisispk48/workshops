Figure 3.5: Principal Components Across Three Covariance Cases

Panel 1 (Positive Covariance, +2.0): PC1 along y = x (45°), capturing 90.0% of total variance (λ₁ = 4.50). PC2 captures 10.0% (λ₂ = 0.50).
Panel 2 (Negative Covariance, -2.0): PC1 along y = -x (-45°), capturing 90.0% of total variance (λ₁ = 4.50). The spine has tilted 90°!
Panel 3 (Zero Covariance, 0.0): Both orthogonal axes have equal eigenvalues (λ₁ = λ₂ = 2.00, 50.0% / 50.0%). There is NO single dominant spine!
