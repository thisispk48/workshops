Figure 3.3: Why Maximize Variance? Worst Line (PC2 Clumping) vs. Best Line (PC1 Spread)

Left (Worst Direction - PC2 at 135°): Points collapse into a dense, uninformative clump with Var = λ₂ = 0.50. Individuals collide (Person A & D both get -0.71; Person B & C both get +0.71), destroying individual distinguishability.
Right (Optimal Direction - PC1 at 45°): Data spreads widely across 9 units with Var = λ₁ = 4.50 (retaining 90% of total variance). Every individual is distinctly separated with minimal error drop lines!
