Figure 2.3: Diagonal Matrix Transformation D = [[3, 0], [0, 2]]

Notice the unified color scheme: [1, 0]ᵀ (Medium Blue) and [0, 1]ᵀ (Light Blue) lie on the invariant eigenspace lines (Red and Green) and preserve their direction. In contrast, [1, 1]ᵀ (Dark Navy Blue) visibly tilts to [3, 2]ᵀ.

Vector [1, 0]ᵀ (Medium Blue): Scales 3× to [3, 0]ᵀ (Direction preserved on Red line)
Vector [0, 1]ᵀ (Light Blue): Scales 2× to [0, 2]ᵀ (Direction preserved on Green line)
Vector [1, 1]ᵀ (Dark Navy): Lands at [3, 2]ᵀ (Direction changed!)
Eigen-axis 1 (Red): y = 0 (λ₁ = 3)
Eigen-axis 2 (Green): x = 0 (λ₂ = 2)
