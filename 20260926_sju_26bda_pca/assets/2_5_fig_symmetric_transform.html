Figure 2.5: Symmetric Matrix Transformation S = [[3, 1], [1, 3]]

Notice the profound contrast with non-diagonal matrices: The eigenvectors q₁ = [1, 1]ᵀ (Red) and q₂ = [-1, 1]ᵀ (Green) are strictly perpendicular at 90° (q₁ · q₂ = 0) on BOTH the left and right! The symmetric matrix stretches space into a rigid rotated orthogonal coordinate system.

e₁ = [1, 0]ᵀ (Medium Blue): Lands at [3, 1]ᵀ
e₂ = [0, 1]ᵀ (Light Blue): Lands at [1, 3]ᵀ
d = [1, 1]ᵀ (Navy Blue): Coincides with q₁
Eigenvector q₁ = [1, 1]ᵀ (Red): Scales 4× to [4, 4]ᵀ (Line y = x)
Eigenvector q₂ = [-1, 1]ᵀ (Green): Scales 2× to [-2, 2]ᵀ (Line y = -x, 90° to q₁)
